Nyxlo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

The short version: Nyxlo doesn't have accounts, doesn't run analytics or trackers, and doesn't send anything about you off your device. The word bank and audio are bundled in the app itself, so it works fully offline. The only outside party involved at all is Google Play, when you choose to buy Nyxlo Premium.

What Nyxlo collects

Nothing that identifies you. Nyxlo doesn't ask for a name, email, or account of any kind, and doesn't include analytics, advertising, or crash-tracking software.

The app does store a small set of preferences — your chosen session length, voice, pacing, and ambient sound settings — but this is saved locally on your device only, using Android's standard app storage. It's never transmitted anywhere, and it's deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

In-app purchases

Nyxlo Premium is sold as a one-time unlock through Google Play Billing. When you make a purchase, that transaction is handled entirely by Google — Nyxlo never sees or stores your payment details. Google's own privacy policy governs that part of the process; see policies.google.com/privacy.

Nyxlo only stores whether you own the unlock (yes or no) locally on your device, so the app knows which features to show you.

Permissions

Nyxlo requests a small number of Android permissions, each tied directly to a feature:

  • Notifications — shows the playback controls on your lock screen during a session.
  • Wake lock & foreground service — lets audio keep playing when your screen turns off, which is the whole point of a sleep app.

None of these are used to access personal data, location, contacts, camera, or microphone — Nyxlo doesn't request any of those.

Children's privacy

Nyxlo doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it doesn't collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top. Since Nyxlo doesn't collect contact details from users, we're not able to notify you directly — checking back here is the way to stay current.
